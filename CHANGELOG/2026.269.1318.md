Relocate the `check-marketplace` R10 check bed (and the `marketplace-app` box it
deploys) from `opencharly/charly`'s `charly.yml` into this repo, which OWNS the
artifact the bed tests.

**This is the fresh re-land of the closed `opencharly/plugin-marketplace#12`**

threshold), rebased onto current `main` with every outstanding finding fixed.
The auto-close policy requires a fresh branch; the predecessor branch is not

WHY relocate: a check bed must live in the repo that owns what it tests. The
bed runner's dev-tree override (`proc.SelfSuperprojectOverridePair`) points the
bed PROJECT's own superproject's `@github` refs at the local tree. While the bed
sat in `charly`, the `plugin-marketplace` candy was therefore never exercised as
in-development code. Relocated here, the bed composes the LOCAL candy with no
`@github` pin.
